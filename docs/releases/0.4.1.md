`souther examples` reads a good deal more of what a model's rules say. A rule it could not read
was reported as unread and left out of the partitions, the rows it generates and the measures, so
each of these is now measured where it was not, and fewer gaps are reported that are not there.

## What is read now

- Arithmetic on numbers worked out of the input: a product of two inputs, a quotient by a value on
  either side of nought, a remainder of a sum or by an input, a rounded number, the month and the
  day of a date moved by some days, the differences of values taken of one place, and what the
  code points of a string add up to.
- Totals, counts and ends of lists the body builds rather than reads: a list written out, a
  filtered one, what `take` or `drop` keeps, a reversed or reordered list, a total weighed by where
  each element stands, and a fold over a list a `map` or a `flatMap` made of the input's elements.
- What a fold files under each key: the total, the least or the greatest of each key's values,
  also across the lists of an outer list put end to end, and what `Map.get` finds under a key the
  body asks for. A key each case of an element holds at a place of its own is read as one key.
- How many different values a walk gathers into a set with `Set.insert` or `Set.union`, how many
  nodes a search down a tree reaches, and how many different values it collects on the way.
- A relation between two values, or among several, is shown to reach every value it allows, so
  the rows that stand on it are generated and the positions it ties are no longer left unread.
- Conditions on the answer of an injected behavior, an invariant written as the sign of an
  order, and an invariant that calls the language's operations.

Where a reading still stops, the report names the figures it stopped at, each a number an author
could raise. A rule a run may stop in, such as one dividing by a value nothing keeps from nought,
is reported as `rule_may_stop_a_run` where the conditions before it are not shown to rule the stop
out.

## Compatibility

- The adequacy report is still schema version 24, and accepts everything 0.4.0 wrote. It now also
  writes values 0.4.0 did not: the finding kind `rule_may_stop_a_run`, further reasons a condition
  or a relation is not read, further kinds of term with their fields (among them `kept_by` and
  `key_by_case`), the `figures` a stopped reading names, and further words under `weakening`. A
  consumer that validates against the copy of schema 24 shipped with 0.4.0, or treats any of these
  lists as closed, has to accept them.
- A point a value was shown at, where something worked out on the way may stop a run (a product
  of two values nothing bounds, a sum of decimals, a call to another behavior), is now written as
  `the_way_there_may_stop`: counted, and not a gap. 0.4.0 wrote such a point as a gap.
- Reading more of a model changes what is reported about it. A report or a set of generated rows
  checked in for 0.4.0 will differ for the same model: fewer rules unread, other partitions, and
  other rows from `souther examples --generate`.
- A module jar compiled by 0.4.0 is accepted as it is.
